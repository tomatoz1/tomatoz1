## Limits

- This checklist records actions to try, not a promise that GitHub will award a badge.
- Exact thresholds above are community-reported and may change.
- Confirm awards on the live profile after GitHub processes the activity.
- Public Sponsor requires sponsoring and is outside this no-cost plan.
- Arctic Code Vault Contributor and Mars 2020 Contributor are historical awards, not new goals.
- Heart On Your Sleeve and Open Sourcerer are not included because availability is unconfirmed.
- Do not use fake coauthors, artificial accounts, purchased stars, or fabricated accepted answers.
